How a week works

Plan the candidate's seven days on the Diary desk: pick a day, pick what it is, and for a rally, a town hall or a fundraiser, tap the state on the map. Advertising, field offices, staff and polls are on the other desks. When you are ready, lock the plan. The week then happens a day at a time; tap the button at the foot of the screen for the next day.

Orders

Hiring, a new advertisement, new offices and a few other things each use one of the week's orders. A poll does not: polls cost only money.

Reading the map

Plain states lean Democrat and hatched states lean Republican; dotted states are toss-ups. The paler the state, the closer it is. Every margin is also written out, as in "D+3". Tap any state for its file.

Election night

Tap any state to see its counties and how much of its vote is still to come. The count can be paused or sped up from the foot of the screen.

Ads and Landslide Plus

The free game stops for an ad at most three times in a campaign, and some extras (a private poll, a stunt, a rewind) can be had by watching one. Landslide Plus, a one-off purchase (Remove ads, on the title screen), removes the breaks and makes the extras free. If you have bought it before, tap Restore purchase on the same screen.

Ad choices

In the United Kingdom, the European Economic Area and Switzerland the game asks for your consent before showing ads. To change your answer, tap Ad choices on the title screen.

Leaderboards

Sign in to Game Center in the Settings app to put your wins on the leaderboards and see other players' best. Records and Leaderboard are on the title screen.

Language

The game follows your iPhone's language. To choose another, tap the language button on the title screen; the game changes language the next time you open it.

My campaign has gone

The game keeps one campaign at a time, on your device. Starting a new campaign replaces it, and deleting the game deletes it.
