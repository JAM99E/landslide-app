Landslide

Run for President. A strategy game for iPhone.

Twelve weeks to election day. Fifty states, 538 electoral votes, and never enough money or time. Plan the campaign day by day, state by state. Then watch election night come in, county by county, and find out whether the doors of the White House open. Or start in Iowa, and win your party's nomination first.

In English, Spanish, French, German, Italian and Brazilian Portuguese. Free to play, with a few ads that Landslide Plus removes. The game works offline, and the game itself collects nothing about you; the privacy policy says what the ads and Game Center involve.
