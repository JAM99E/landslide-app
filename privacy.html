Landslide

Privacy policy. Last changed 9 October 2026. Landslide · Help

Landslide is a game you play by yourself on your iPhone. It collects nothing about you.

What the game stores

Your campaign in progress is saved in a file on your own device so that you can come back to it. It never leaves the device. Deleting the game deletes it.

What the game sends

Nothing. The game makes no connection to the internet. There are no accounts, no adverts, no analytics and no tracking.

Purchases

If you buy anything in the game, the purchase is handled by Apple. The game is told only that the purchase was made. It never sees your name, your payment details or your Apple Account.

The people in it

The candidates are real nominees shown as historical public figures, with official United States government portraits that are in the public domain. The events of each campaign are invented. Landslide is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, any candidate, campaign, party or broadcaster.
